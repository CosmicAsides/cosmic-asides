Privacy policy: Cosmic Asides Stats

Cosmic Asides Stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the Cosmic Asides channel.

  • It reads the channel's own YouTube and TikTok statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time) with read-only access.
  • It never uploads, edits, deletes or posts anything.
  • The data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's private dashboard. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google or TikTok account settings.
